# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

### Added

- Persistent SQLite-backed karma, statistics, unlocked titles, and active titles
- Configurable karma gains and losses for combat, trading, animal interaction, and villager curing
- Daily decay, PvP cooldowns, mutual-kill detection, mob streak limits, and other anti-farm rules
- Karmic and achievement-based titles with configurable colors and unlock conditions
- Positive and negative karma effects, including combat, economy, movement, experience, and mob behavior changes
- Player commands for viewing, resetting, and administering karma and titles
- English and Russian localization
- Optional PlaceholderAPI expansion for karma and title values
- Configurable Enderman behavior
